# veteran lane

Veteran and military-spouse business funding. Verified 2026-09-24.
Rule: HOH annual watch, Warrior Rising rolling path, OVBD services only.
Quote the notice per task; never promise cash from a services office.

## Hiring Our Heroes Small Business Grant (annual, CLOSED for 2026)

- Awards: 4 x $10K plus 1 x $25K grand prize. 2026 cycle apps due
  2025-12-15; winners spring 2026.
- Cited gates: 51% owner honorably-discharged veteran or military spouse;
  3-20 staff including 1099s; under $5M revenue plus need criteria; 50 states.
- Channel: online application when the window opens.
- URLs: hiringourheroes.org/small-business-grant/ + /rules/
- Action: annual watch. Re-verify terms when the next window posts.

## Warrior Rising (rolling)

- Awards cited $2K-$150K non-dilutive for finalists; VA News track up to $20K.
- Path: Academy to quarterly virtual University (two evenings) to
  invite-only Showers (Cedar Rapids, DC, Salt Lake City); travel covered.
- URLs: warriorrising.org/business-showers/ + /warrior-university/
- Action: rolling entry. Fit for veteran founders who can pitch.

## SBA Office of Veterans Business Development (services, not cash)

- No direct dollars. Funds organizations via B2B / SDVETP / VBOC / VFPETP /
  WVETP on per-NOFO cadence. Veterans use VBOC and Boots to Business.
- URLs: sba.gov OVBD + grants + veterans pages; org NOFOs via SAM.
- Action: referral only. Never list OVBD as a grant source.

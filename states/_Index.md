# State and territory research

Use the [bundled nationwide registry](../grant_engine/data/us-sources.json) for
starting sources in all 50 states, DC and five territories. A route is a place
to research, not a claim that its grants have been collected or verified.

```powershell
python -m grant_engine plan --jurisdiction US --applicant-type nonprofit
python -m grant_engine plan --jurisdiction TX --purpose workforce
```

The three legacy background notes below retain dated research from September
2026. Recheck current official notices before relying on any amount, deadline,
eligibility condition or open status:

- [Ohio](ohio.md)
- [Pennsylvania](pennsylvania.md)
- [Texas](texas.md)

Use [_Template.md](_Template.md) for a new researched note. Choose agencies by
applicant and project, then expand relevant county, municipal, workforce, tribal,
utility and private administrators. Record blocked and unattempted sources.
See [coverage limits](../docs/COVERAGE.md) and [research workflow](../find.md).

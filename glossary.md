# glossary

Plain-English definitions. No jargon without an entry here.

- BASC: Business Assistance Service Center. Regional office (PA has 7)
  giving no-cost coaching, business planning, and financing prep. The
  front door to state microgrant rounds; counselors see terms first.
- CDFI: Community Development Financial Institution. Local lender for
  underserved businesses. PA's network (17 CDFIs, 67 counties) runs the
  microgrant and loan programs with DCED money.
- DCED: PA Department of Community and Economic Development. The state
  money behind BASC and microgrants.
- CFDA / Assistance Listings: catalog numbers (example 93.143) tagging
  federal programs. SAM.gov Assistance Listings is the searchable home.
- SBIR / STTR: federal R and D awards for tech small businesses, phased
  (Phase I proves, Phase II builds). R and D only, not operating cash.
- UEI: Unique Entity ID from SAM.gov. Required for federal awards.
- NOFO: Notice of Funding Opportunity. The notice text is law; blog
  summaries are hearsay.
- HDBA: Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance (PA). The $14M+
  umbrella over BASC + microgrants.
- SSBCI: State Small Business Credit Initiative. Mostly loans, not grants.

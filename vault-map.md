# vault map

How vault-mapped businesses plug into legends grant. Pro-group members
already carry their business in vault form (AI Marketing Hub library
ontology, Empire shelf notes). This module reads that map instead of
re-interviewing the owner every time.

## Reading a mapped business

1. Take the vault path the person names (their business note or client
   folder). That path is the source of truth for identity facts.
2. Extract only the `match.md` must-haves into a `profiles/<slug>.md`
   copy. Quote or cite the vault note per field; keep the vault path
   recorded in the profile.
3. Where the vault is silent (revenue, headcount, NAICS, UEI), ask once.
4. Multi-client: repeat per client. Shared registry, separate profiles,
   separate opportunity notes prefixed by client slug.

## Ontology minimum (what the vault note should hold)

Identity, offerings, NAICS-adjacent description, geography, team size,
revenue band, ownership flags, proof assets (formation, financials,
testimonials, metrics). Anything missing just becomes one intake question.

## Living link

When the vault business note changes, refresh the profile's affected
fields and re-run `match.md` scoring for open opportunities. Stale
profiles produce stale shortlists; the profile carries an Updated date.
